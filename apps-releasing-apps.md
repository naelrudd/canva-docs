# Releasing apps

How to release an app to the public or a team.

## Public apps

To release a public app:

1. Navigate to the app via the Developer Portal.
2. On the **Release app** page, select **Release app**.
3. In the confirmation dialog, select **Release app**.

The app will immediately become discoverable in the Canva editor but will not become discoverable in the [Apps Marketplace](https://www.canva.com/apps) until it's manually added by a Canva employee, which typically happens within a week.

## Team apps

**Team apps do not have a separate release step.** When the owner or an administrator of the team approves the app, it immediately becomes available to members of that team.
