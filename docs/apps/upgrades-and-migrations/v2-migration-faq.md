# Apps SDK v2 Upgrade FAQ

Some important things to know about upgrading to Apps SDK v2.

## What's changing?

Many APIs are being deprecated and introduced. See the [Upgrade guide](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/upgrades-and-migrations/v2-migration-guide/).

## Do I have to upgrade my app?

Yes. All developers must upgrade their app(s) to v2.

## How long do I have to upgrade?

12 months, starting from September 25th, 2024. The upgraded app should be released by September 25th, 2025.

## How do I upgrade my app?

See the [Upgrade guide](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/upgrades-and-migrations/v2-migration-guide/).

## What happens if I don't upgrade?

Your app(s) will be disabled and removed from the [Apps Marketplace](https://www.canva.com/your-apps) after September 25th, 2025.

## Can I still create v1 apps?

No. All new apps must use v2.

## Can I launch my existing v1 app?

Yes, but only until the end of 2024.

## Can I use a combination of v1 and v2 APIs?

No. The APIs are not interoperable.
