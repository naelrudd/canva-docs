# Managing team apps

How to manage team apps as a team owner or administrator.

AVAILABILITY: Team apps are only available to teams on an [Enterprise plan](https://www.canva.com/enterprise/).

## Reviewing team apps

1. Navigate to **Account settings > Team apps** page.
2. Identify the app to review.
3. Open the app in the Canva editor by selecting **Preview**.

## Releasing team apps

1. Navigate to **Account settings > Team apps** page.
2. Identify the app to release.
3. Select **Release**.
4. Confirm.

## Rejecting team apps

1. Navigate to **Account settings > Team apps** page.
2. Identify the app to reject.
3. Select **Reject**.
4. Explain why and confirm.

## Disabling team apps

1. Navigate to **Account settings > Team apps** page.
2. Identify the app to disable.
3. Select **Disable**.
4. Explain why and confirm.
