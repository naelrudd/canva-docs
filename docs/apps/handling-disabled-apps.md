# Disabled apps

What to do if your app is disabled.

## Public apps

Disabled by Canva's app review team for reasons such as security issues, backend unresponsiveness, or significant bugs.

### How to re-enable

**Scenario #1** (frontend/configuration change required):
1. Create a new version of the app.
2. Make the change.
3. Submit for review.

**Scenario #2** (only backend change required):
1. Make the change without creating a new version.
2. Inform Canva via the JSD ticket.

## Team apps

Disabled by the team owner or administrator.

### How to re-enable

Same scenarios as public apps, but the team owner/administrator handles the re-enabling.
