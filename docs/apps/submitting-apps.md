# Submitting apps

An introduction to the app submission process.

## How to submit apps for review

### Public apps

1. [Generate a bundle](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/bundling-apps/) of the app's source code.
2. Navigate to the app via the Developer Portal.
3. On the **Code upload** page, upload the bundle.
4. On the **App listing details** pages, provide written content and visual assets.
5. On the **Testing instructions** page, provide login credentials if needed.
6. Within **App status** on the **Submit app** page, agree to terms and select **Submit app**.

### Team apps

1. [Generate a bundle](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/bundling-apps/) of the app's source code.
2. Navigate to the app via the Developer Portal.
3. Upload the bundle and provide listing details.
4. Select **Submit app**.

## How to modify submitted apps

After an app has been submitted for review, it enters a **Submitted** state and can't be modified. Cancel the submission to make changes.

## How to cancel app submissions

1. Navigate to the app via the Developer Portal.
2. Within **App status** on the **Submit app** page, select **Cancel submission**.
