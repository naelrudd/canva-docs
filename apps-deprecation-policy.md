# Deprecation policy

How we deprecate features in the Apps SDK.

## TL;DR

* This policy only applies to the public, stable version of the Apps SDK.
* When a feature is deprecated, it remains available for at least a further 6 months.
* Canva communicates deprecations at multiple points via multiple channels.
* There may be exceptions for security incidents.

## Applicability

Applies to the public, stable version. Private/beta APIs may change at any moment without warning.

## Deprecation period

6 months.

## Communication

Via email, developer community, documentation, and Developer Portal.

## Breaking vs. non-breaking changes

Breaking: Adding required properties, renaming methods/types, removing enum values.
Non-breaking: Adding methods, adding optional properties, adding enum values.

## Exceptions

Security issues may bypass the deprecation period.
