# App icon guidelines

Learn how to design the perfect icon for your app.

* **Keep it simple:** Use a unique concept or element and avoid adding too many details.
* **Use graphic images instead of photos:** Create graphics to highlight key features.
* **Design it in a full-bleed square image:** Use 1:1 ratio, 512px x 512px.
* **Avoid adding text to your icon:** Minimize text unless essential.

NOTE: Only use content that you have the rights to use.

## Do

- DO incorporate recognizable brand elements
- DO use graphics reflective of the app's output
- DO opt for split-screen layout for before/after states
- DO exaggerate output details for recognition
- DO consider legibility at smaller scale
- DO prioritize clean lines and forms
- DO choose complementary background colors
- DO pair intricate backgrounds with simple foregrounds

## Don't

- DON'T overcrowd with unnecessary details
- DON'T use low-contrast colors or thin lines
- DON'T add border radius to the background
- DON'T use cultural references that aren't universally understood
- DON'T use transparent backgrounds
- DON'T make icon elements larger than the keyline guide
- DON'T use photos that lose detail when scaled down
- DON'T use text unless it's part of the logo
