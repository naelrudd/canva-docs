# Canva Apps and Canva Integrations

Explaining the differences between third-party additions to Canva.

## Canva Apps

Canva Apps available in the [Canva Apps Marketplace](https://www.canva.com/your-apps/) are developed using the [Canva Apps SDK](/docs/apps). These apps are available to users within the Canva editor, allowing app developers to add new or improved functionally to Canva.
