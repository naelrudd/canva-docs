# App review process

An overview of how apps are reviewed.

## Public apps

Reviewed by Canva's dedicated team of app reviewers.

### Review criteria

Public apps must adhere to requirements explained throughout the documentation and the [submission checklist](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/submission-checklist/).

### Outcomes

* **Approved**: The app can be released on-demand by the owner.
* **Rejected**: The owner receives feedback explaining required changes.

## Team apps

Reviewed by the owner or an administrator of the team. Canva is not involved.

### Outcomes

* **Approved**: The app is immediately released to team members.
* **Rejected**: The owner receives feedback via the Developer Portal.

## Known limitations

* In the Developer Portal, only the most recent feedback is visible.
