# Canva Designs, Folders, and Assets

Explanations of some key Canva resources.

## Designs

A design is anything you create in Canva, including:

* [Documents](https://www.canva.com/docs/)
* [Presentations](https://www.canva.com/presentations/)
* [Print products](https://www.canva.com/print/), such as T-shirts, mugs, and business cards
* [Social media content](https://www.canva.com/social-media/), such as Instagram posts
* [Videos](https://www.canva.com/video-editor/)
* [Websites](https://www.canva.com/website-builder/)
* [Whiteboards](https://www.canva.com/whiteboards/)

This doesn't include elements within a design, such as images and videos uploaded by a user or built-in elements like shapes and stock images.

Most Canva designs are between 40x40 pixels and 8,000x8,000 pixels and can have multiple pages.
